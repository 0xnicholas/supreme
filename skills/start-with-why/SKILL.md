# Start with Why

## Overview

**People don't buy what you do; they buy why you do it.** — Simon Sinek

The Golden Circle shows that inspiring leaders communicate inside-out: **Why** (purpose) → **How** (process) → **What** (product).

```
         ╱  WHAT   ╲
        ╱   HOW    ╲
       ╱    WHY    ╲
```

| Level | Meaning | Example (Apple) |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| **WHY** | Purpose, cause, belief | Challenge the status quo |
| **HOW** | Actions that bring Why to life | Beautiful design, simple UX |
| **WHAT** | Products/services | Mac, iPhone, iPad |

**Most communicate outside-in:** "We make great computers. Want one?"
**Inspiring leaders communicate inside-out:** "We believe in challenging the status quo. We do that with beautiful design. We happen to make great computers."

---

## Key Concepts

### Celery Test

A decision filter: if an opportunity doesn't serve your Why, say no — even if it's profitable.

> New feature doesn't advance your mission? → No.
> Investor wants you to pivot away from your purpose? → No.

### Law of Diffusion

Ideas spread through: Innovators (2.5%) → Early Adopters (13.5%) → Early Majority (34%) → Late Majority (34%) → Laggards (16%).

The first 15-18% buy **Why**, not **What**. Start with believers, not the mass market.

### Clarity · Discipline · Consistency

| Element | Without it |
|---------|-----------|
| **Clarity** of Why | Chasing every opportunity |
| **Discipline** of How | Diluting your values |
| **Consistency** of action | Losing trust |

## Find Your Why

1. Collect **5-10 stories** of when you felt most fulfilled
2. Identify **recurring themes** (helping others? fighting unfairness? building?)
3. Distill into a single sentence: *"To [verb] [someone] to [contribute/value]"*

> A venture cannot have a stronger Why than its founder.

## Quick Reference

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Defining the venture | Start with Why → How → What (inside-out) |
| Evaluating a feature | Apply Celery Test |
| Writing a pitch | Lead with belief, then process, then product |
| Hiring | Do they believe what you believe? |
| Early traction | Target Innovators + Early Adopters (first 15-18%) |

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ "Why is just a mission statement" — Why must drive decisions, not decorate a wall
- ❌ "Why changes as we grow" — The Why doesn't change; How and What evolve
- ❌ "We'll figure out Why later" — Without Why, you attract price-sensitive customers, not believers
